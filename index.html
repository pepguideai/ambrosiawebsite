FREQUENTLY ASKED

Before you order

ALL QUESTIONS →

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, and USDC, BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and LTC. Card payments are processed through a third-party provider, so Ambrosia never stores or directly handles your card information. Cryptocurrency orders are released for dispatch after two network confirmations.

What counts as a qualifying order?

Any order that has been properly verified under our Terms of Sale. Verification is the condition, not an order value — the $200 threshold applies to free shipping only. One welcome offer per account, valid for 30 days from the date you sign up, and not combinable with other offers.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are generally processed within 2–3 business days. Once a package leaves our facility we are not responsible for delays caused by couriers or by an incorrect address supplied at checkout. Estimated domestic delivery is a further 2–5 business days after dispatch.

Do you offer refunds or returns?

Given the nature of the materials, all sales are final. Refunds are issued at our discretion and only where material arrives incorrect, damaged, or is shown to be defective. Issues must be reported within 7 calendar days of receipt, by email to info@ambrosiastandard.com.

What documentation ships with an order?

Production and handling records covering the lot, and, once testing is complete, its certificate of analysis.

Will you ask me to prove it?

We may. Ambrosia reserves the right to request proof of qualifications, to decline any order, and to cancel an order at our discretion at any point before dispatch.
