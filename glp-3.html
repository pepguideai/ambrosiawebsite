INTENDED USE

GLP-3 is a synthetic peptide research reagent supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research, testing and analytical work. It is not intended for human or animal administration, and it must not be used for diagnostic, therapeutic or clinical purposes. Ambrosia makes no claim regarding safety, efficacy or suitability for any such use.

STORAGE & HANDLING
Store the lyophilised powder at −20 °C
Once reconstituted for laboratory analysis, store at 2–8 °C
Avoid repeated freeze and thaw cycles
Maintain aseptic technique throughout handling
Sealed vials carry 24 months from the date of manufacture when held at −20 °C. Once reconstituted, hold at 2–8 °C and use within 28 days.
