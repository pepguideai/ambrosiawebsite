Acceptance of orders

An order is an offer to purchase. A contract is formed only when Ambrosia accepts and dispatches the order. Ambrosia may decline any order at its discretion.

Eligibility

By placing an order you confirm you are 21 years of age or older and are acquiring the materials for laboratory research only, as set out in the Research Use Policy.

Pricing and payment

Prices are stated in United States dollars and exclude shipping and any applicable tax.

Payment is taken when the order is placed, by credit or debit card or in USDC, BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP or LTC. Card payments are handled by a third-party processor; Ambrosia does not store or directly handle card details. Cryptocurrency orders are released for dispatch after two network confirmations.

Where a price is listed in error, Ambrosia may cancel the order and refund in full rather than supply at the incorrect price.

Availability

Catalogue listings do not guarantee stock. Where a compound cannot be supplied, Ambrosia will notify the purchaser and refund any sum already taken for it.

Documentation

Each lot is supplied with its production and handling records and, once testing is complete, its certificate of analysis. Certificates are published per lot on the compound’s page.

To be drafted by counsel. Ambrosia’s position is that materials are supplied as described and tested, with no warranty of fitness for any particular purpose, and that liability is limited to the price paid for the affected material.
