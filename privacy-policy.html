Privacy Policy

What Ambrosia collects, why we collect it, how long we hold it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

LAST UPDATED 13 SEPTEMBER 2026

Who this covers

Ambrosia Research Supply Company, LLC, a Tennessee limited liability company based in Nashville, operates ambrosiastandard.com and the checkout hosted alongside it. This policy describes what that site collects, why, how long it is held, and what you can ask us to do with it.

It applies to visitors and customers. It does not apply to anything a third party does on its own site, including a payment provider or a courier once a shipment is handed over.

What we collect

Only what an order, an eligibility record, or a reply to your enquiry actually requires.

ORDER AND CONTACT DETAILS
Name, email address, shipping and billing address, telephone number where you provide one, and your order history. Collected at checkout.
ELIGIBILITY ATTESTATIONS
The two confirmations you give before an order is accepted — that you are 21 or older, and that the materials are for laboratory research only — together with the date and time in UTC, the IP address the confirmation came from, and the version of the terms shown to you. This is a compliance record and is kept with the order.
MARKETING CONSENT
If you tick the marketing box at checkout, that choice and the address it applies to. An email address entered to reveal a welcome code is not sent to us — it stays in your own browser.
PARTNERSHIP ENQUIRIES
Where a distributor or institutional partnership is discussed by email, the contact details and business information provided in that correspondence.
TECHNICAL AND LOG DATA
IP address, browser and device type, pages requested and timestamps — recorded by our hosting providers in the ordinary course of serving the site, and used for security, fraud prevention and diagnostics.

We do not ask for, and have no use for, a date of birth, a government identification number, or any health information. Do not send us any.

What we never see

Card numbers are entered into Stripe's systems, not ours. Ambrosia never receives, stores or processes full card details — only the confirmation that a payment succeeded and the last digits needed to reconcile it.

Cryptocurrency payments are settled on a public blockchain through the processor handling that payment method. We hold no wallet keys and take no custody of funds. Be aware that blockchain transactions are, by their nature, publicly visible and permanent; that is a property of the network, not a disclosure by us.

Why we collect it

To accept, verify and fulfil an order. To confirm that a purchaser is eligible, and to hold a record that the confirmation was made. To answer enquiries. To assess a partnership enquiry. To detect and prevent fraud and abuse. To meet our own record-keeping obligations.

The eligibility record exists because of what we sell. It is the evidence that a purchaser attested to their age and to a research-only purpose at the moment of sale, and it is retained for that reason alone.

What is stored in your browser

The storefront keeps your cart, your gate confirmation, and whether you have seen or claimed the welcome offer in your own browser's local storage. None of it is transmitted to us. The welcome offer asks only for a confirmation, not for contact details. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

The checkout, hosted on WordPress and WooCommerce, sets the session and cart cookies it needs to function. These are strictly necessary to complete an order.

Analytics and advertising tooling: none is in use at the time of writing. If any is added, it will be named in this section and, where consent is required, requested before it loads.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Data reaches a third party only where that party is needed to run the business, and only to the extent it needs:

HOSTING
The static storefront is served by Vercel. The store, checkout and order records run on WordPress hosting provided by Hostinger.
PAYMENTS
Card payments are processed by Stripe, which receives what it needs to take payment and to meet its own anti-fraud and financial obligations. Stripe holds the card details; we do not.
SHIPPING
Carriers receive the name, address and contact details necessary to deliver a package.
EMAIL
Marketing consent and the address it applies to are held in WooCommerce alongside the order, not passed to an outside mailing platform. If one is introduced, it will be named here.
PROFESSIONAL AND LEGAL
Our accountants and counsel, where they need it. Law enforcement or a regulator, where we are legally required to respond to a valid request.

If Ambrosia is ever sold or merged, customer records may transfer as part of that transaction. Notice would be given here before it took effect.

Marketing

Marketing email is sent only to people who asked for it. The checkbox at checkout is unticked by default and consent is never a condition of purchase — an order is not permission to market.

The welcome code is the same for everyone and is revealed in your browser, so an address entered there never reaches us. We receive it only if you tick the box at checkout. Every marketing message carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect on all marketing email. Transactional messages about an order you placed are not marketing and will continue.

We do not currently send marketing text messages. If SMS is introduced, consent will be collected separately and this section will state the terms.

How long we keep it

ORDER AND ELIGIBILITY RECORDS
Seven years from the order date. This is the ordinary business-records horizon, and it covers the compliance purpose these records exist for.
MARKETING ADDRESSES
Until you unsubscribe, plus a short suppression record so we do not contact you again in error.
PARTNERSHIP ENQUIRIES
Twelve months from the decision, unless the partnership goes ahead.
SERVER LOGS
As retained by our hosting providers in the ordinary course, typically weeks rather than years.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy of it, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Write to info@ambrosiastandard.com and we will respond within 45 days.

Two limits, stated plainly. We cannot delete an eligibility record or an order record while we are required to retain it, and we will tell you when that is the reason. And because those records are the evidence of a lawful sale, a deletion request does not remove them from an order already placed.

Tennessee residents have these rights under the Tennessee Information Protection Act, including the right to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, and profiling. We do none of those things. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision; we will respond to an appeal within 60 days and tell you how to contact the Tennessee Attorney General if you remain unsatisfied.

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states with comparable statutes have equivalent rights, and we handle every request to the same standard rather than asking where you live first.

Age and eligibility

This site is not directed to anyone under 21 and orders are accepted only from qualified purchasers acting in a research capacity. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a minor has given us information, write to info@ambrosiastandard.com and it will be deleted.

Security

The site and checkout are served over TLS. Access to order records is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfect, and we will not claim otherwise — if a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

International visitors

Ambrosia operates from the United States, ships only to addresses within the United States, and processes information here. If you visit from outside the United States, information such as server logs is processed in the United States, where privacy law may differ from your own.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the date below the title changes with it and the substance of the change is described here. Continuing to use the site after that means the revised policy applies.
