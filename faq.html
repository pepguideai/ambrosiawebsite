THE ONE QUESTION WE GET MOST

Why are some of the compounds blue?

The blue colour comes from copper. Any compound containing GHK-Cu is blue because GHK-Cu is a copper-coordinated tripeptide — glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine holding a copper(II) ion — and copper(II) in that coordination absorbs the warm end of the spectrum and reflects blue. The colour is the chemistry, not a coating or an additive. Nothing is dyed, tinted or corrected: where a photograph on this site shows blue, blue is what arrived.

GHK-Cu is blue on its own, and the Glow Blend and Klow Blend are blue because each contains GHK-Cu. The other compounds in the catalogue contain no copper and are white to off-white lyophilised powders. In practice the colour works as a table of contents: blue means copper is present.

Colour is not a purity test. Hue tells you copper is in the formulation; it says nothing about concentration, identity or contamination. That is what the certificate of analysis is for.
